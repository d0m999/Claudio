RESONANT-BOWL AUDIO PACKAGE LICENSE DECLARATION
Captured: 2026-09-02

The five resonant-bowl audio files are original deterministic procedural
renderings made for Claudio. The generator uses mathematical oscillators,
inharmonic partials, envelopes, a soft synthetic strike transient, and short
delays only. It contains no third-party recording, sample, field recording,
melody recording, or licensed audio source, and does not copy a particular
bowl performance.
