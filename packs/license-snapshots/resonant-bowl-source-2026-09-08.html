Source

This pack is rendered locally by scripts/generate-resonant-bowl.py. It uses deterministic mathematical synthesis: inharmonic bowl-like partials, a soft synthetic strike transient, a restrained pitch bloom, a short room return, and per-tone fades applied before contributions enter the shared mix buffer. It contains no third-party recording or sample and does not copy a particular bowl performance.

Event recipe

  • task_start: C4 bowl touch followed by a quieter G4 resonance bloom; the first 320 ms carries the onset, the next 260 ms is a continuous natural release, and the final 40 ms is explicit digital silence within the 1.15 s event.
  • stop: one full D4 resonance with the longest natural tail, 1.84 s.
  • stop_failure: damped A4 to F4 to D4 descending phrase, 1.42 s.
  • notification: two separated G4 bowl touches, 1.38 s.
  • subagent_stop: one low G3 acknowledgement with a short body and tail, 740 ms.

The four lifecycle events share a -0.8 dBFS generator peak target. The rendered files are normalized with the same peak method before MP3 encoding.
